Product Page – Floating Add to Cart

Displays the product image, name, price, and Add to Cart or Select Options button in a floating bar.

Appears after scrolling past the original Add to Cart section.

Disappears when scrolling back to the original Add to Cart section.

Uses the original Zen Cart Add to Cart functionality.

Keeps the selected quantity and product attributes.

Automatically updates the displayed price when the product price changes.

Add to Cart: For products without required options, adds the product to the cart using the selected quantity.

Select Options: For products with attributes (such as color, size, or style), displays a Select Options button instead of Add to Cart. Clicking it smoothly scrolls the customer back to the product attributes section (#productAttributes) so they can choose their options before adding the product to the cart.

Prevents customers from accidentally adding products without selecting required attributes.

Supports desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

Works alongside the Back to Top button without overlapping it on mobile.

Purpose: Makes purchasing easier by keeping the product information and purchase button accessible while customers scroll through the product page, without interfering with Zen Cart's existing product options or checkout functionality.

Mobile Cart Swipe-to-Delete

Easy Drag-and-Drop Installation!
Simply drag and drop the included files into their corresponding Zen Cart template folders.
Once installed, customers can swipe left on mobile cart items to reveal a Delete button for quick and easy removal.


Visit https://www.zencartdemo.com to see the Mobile Cart Swipe-to-Delete feature in action!

Simply resize your browser window to mobile width (under 576px), add a product to your cart, and swipe or drag the item to the left to reveal the Delete button.

You can also press F12 in Chrome or Firefox and switch to mobile view to test the feature.
